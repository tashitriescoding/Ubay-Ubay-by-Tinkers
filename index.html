Be a part of the program

We are inviting you to design a button pin bundle for Ubay-Ubay.

You make the art. We print, pack, and sell it, and you earn a cut of every sale. Your bundle goes to the merch table for FASO.

How to join

Design 4 pins

Four pin designs together make 1 bundle. Send each pin as a PNG, 1000 x 1000 px.

4 pin designs. Together they make 1 bundle.

A bundle name logo. It goes at the top of the card.

The backcard is on us. A FASO artist designs it.

A free bundle for you. We make copies of your bundle, and 1 free copy goes to you.

Deadline: the last day of every month. The month after, the team makes your pin bundle.

Pin template

Keep your art inside the red line. The area up to the gray line wraps around the side of the pin. Anything past the gray line will not show.

What you earn

Every sale of your bundle or solo pin pays you a share. The rest goes to Tinkers, the UP Cebu Fine Arts org behind Ubay-Ubay.

Bundle, ₱240: you get ₱100, Tinkers gets ₱140

Solo pin, ₱50: you get ₱10, Tinkers gets ₱40

Payout timing and method will be announced by email before your first sale.

Questions?

When is the deadline?

The last day of every month. Upload your designs with your upload link by then. The month after, the team prints and packs your bundle.

Can I make more than 4 designs?

Yes, but only 4 designs go into one bundle. 4 designs make 1 bundle.

Can I also send single pins?

Yes, as solo pins, if you also submitted a bundle.

Who designs the backcard?

A FASO artist makes every backcard, so all bundles look the same.

How is each sale split?

A bundle sells for ₱240: ₱100 to you, ₱140 to Tinkers.

How to join:

  1. Read and sign the acceptance letter.
  2. Wait for our email. We reply within 1 to 3 business days to let you know if you're in.
  3. Upload your designs on the website by the last day of the month.
